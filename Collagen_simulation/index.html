01. Project Overview

Graph Abstraction

Modeling collagen fibrils as complex network graphs where nodes represent tropocollagen molecules and edges represent intermolecular cross-links.

Perturbation Dynamics

Simulating mechanical and biochemical stressors via localized network perturbations and observing stress propagation paths.

Quantum Optimization

Utilizing QAOA-inspired algorithms to find the most energetically efficient repair pathways within the degraded lattice structure.

03. Core Algorithms

Hamiltonian Formulation

Mapping the lattice repair problem to an Ising model Hamiltonian for quantum optimization.

$$ H = \sum J_{ij} Z_i Z_j + \sum h_i Z_i $$

QAOA Ansatz

The parameterized quantum circuit used to explore the solution space of the repair Hamiltonian.

$$ |\gamma,\beta\rangle = \prod e^{-i \beta_k H_B} e^{-i \gamma_k H_P} |+\rangle^n $$

Stress Propagation

Differential equation governing how mechanical stress cascades through intact cross-links.

$$ \frac{dS_i}{dt} = -\gamma S_i + \alpha \sum A_{ij}(S_j - S_i) $$

Coherence Metric

A macroscopic measure of tissue health derived from microscopic lattice integrity.

$$ C = \frac{1}{N} \sum (1 - D_i) \cdot \frac{\deg(i)}{\text{max\_deg}} $$
